## Chapter 9: Statistics Dashboard

Click the Graph icon in the sidebar to open the Statistics Dashboard. This screen helps you understand your learning habits.

### Why Check Statistics?
Studying can feel like walking in the dark. Statistics turn on the light. They prove to you that your memory is actually improving over time.

### The Review Heatmap
- **What it is**: A grid of tiny colored squares, just like GitHub. Every square represents a day of the year.
- **How to read it**: The darker the green square, the more cards you studied that day. Gray squares mean you didn't study at all.
- **Why it exists**: To motivate you! Trying to keep a "streak" (studying every single day without breaking the chain) is one of the best ways to build a habit.

### KPI Cards (The Big Numbers)
At the top of the dashboard, you will see four important numbers:
1. **Total Reviews**: The total number of button presses (Good, Hard, etc.) you have ever made.
2. **Current Streak**: How many days in a row you have studied. Don't break it!
3. **Retention Rate**: The percentage of cards you successfully remembered (hit Good/Easy on). You want this number to be around 85% to 90%. If it's 100%, your cards are too easy!
4. **Average Review Time**: How many seconds it takes you, on average, to answer a card.

### FSRS Memory State
- **What it is**: A breakdown of the math behind your learning.
- **Mature vs. Young Cards**: "Young" cards are new cards you are still learning. "Mature" cards are cards you have proven you know well (they aren't due for 21 days or more). Watching your Mature card count grow is the ultimate proof that you are learning!

## Chapter 10: AI Assistant

Click the Robot Brain icon in the sidebar to open the AI Copilot. 

### What it is
The AI Assistant is a smart chat window built directly into NeoCards. It can read your flashcards and help you learn.

### Privacy First (Local AI vs Cloud AI)
- **Local AI**: If you have a powerful computer, NeoCards can run the AI entirely on your machine (using Ollama). This means your notes never leave your computer. 100% private.
- **Cloud AI**: If you want a smarter, faster AI, you can connect NeoCards to OpenAI or Anthropic. This sends your text to the cloud. You choose what works best for you in the Settings.

### Example Workflows (When to use it)

#### 1. Generate Flashcards from a Textbook
- Copy a long paragraph from a website or textbook.
- Paste it into the AI chat.
- Type: *"Turn this text into 5 simple flashcards."*
- The AI will generate the cards, and you can click the **[Add to Deck]** button right inside the chat to save them!

#### 2. Explain a Difficult Concept
- If a flashcard is confusing, open the AI chat.
- Type: *"Explain the mitochondria to me like I am a 5-year-old."*
- The AI will break it down into simple terms.

#### 3. Summarize a Deck
- Type: *"Summarize all my notes about the French Revolution."*
- Because NeoCards has a "Retrieval Engine," the AI actually searches your SQLite database, reads your French Revolution notes, and writes a summary based *only* on what you have studied.

## Chapter 11: Plugins

NeoCards is a "Platform." This means other software developers can write mini-programs (Plugins) that add brand new features to NeoCards.

### How to Install a Plugin
1. Go to Settings (the Gear icon).
2. Click the **Plugins** tab.
3. You will see a list of available plugins. Click **Install** next to the one you want.
4. Click **Enable** to turn it on.

### Permissions (Keeping your data safe)
NeoCards uses a "Sandbox." This means plugins are locked in a cage and cannot steal your data.
- When you enable a plugin, a warning box might appear: *"This plugin wants to read your Database."*
- If it's a plugin that generates Statistics, you should click **Allow**.
- If it's a Pomodoro Timer plugin, it doesn't need to read your database. NeoCards will block it automatically if it tries!

### Example Plugins
- **Pomodoro Timer**: Adds a 25-minute countdown clock to your study screen.
- **Heatmap**: Adds a visual streak graph directly to your Home screen.
- **Advanced Math**: Adds support for extremely complex physics drawing tools inside the Editor.

## Chapter 12: Synchronization

Syncing allows you to study on your desktop, and then open your laptop later and pick up exactly where you left off.

### How Sync Works (Local First)
Most web apps stop working when you lose Wi-Fi. NeoCards is different. 
- **Offline Mode**: You can create 500 cards and review 1000 cards while sitting on an airplane with zero internet. 
- **The Journal**: Every time you make a change, NeoCards writes it down in a hidden "Journal" on your hard drive. 
- **Automatic Sync**: As soon as your computer reconnects to Wi-Fi, NeoCards silently uploads the Journal in the background. You never have to click a "Save to Cloud" button.

### Conflict Resolution
What happens if you edit the exact same flashcard on your Desktop AND your Laptop while both are offline?
- When both connect to the internet, NeoCards looks at the timestamp. The computer that saved the card most recently "wins," and its version is kept. 
- *Best Practice*: Try to let NeoCards sync before closing your laptop lid to avoid conflicts!

### The Sync Dashboard
- If you click the Cloud icon in the sidebar, you can see a live view of your Sync Journal. It will show you exactly how many changes are waiting to be uploaded.
- You can click **Sync Now** to force a sync immediately.
