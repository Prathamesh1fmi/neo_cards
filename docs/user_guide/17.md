## Chapter 17: Frequently Asked Questions

Here are 100 common questions to help you master NeoCards.

### General & Setup
1. **Is NeoCards free?** Yes, the core desktop application is completely free.
2. **Do I need the internet to study?** No. NeoCards is "Offline First". You only need the internet to sync.
3. **Can I use NeoCards on my phone?** Currently, NeoCards is a desktop-first application optimized for Windows, Mac, and Linux.
4. **Where are my flashcards stored?** They are stored safely on your computer's hard drive in a hidden folder inside your user directory.
5. **How do I update NeoCards?** NeoCards has an auto-updater. It will prompt you when a new version is available.
6. **How do I uninstall NeoCards?** Use your operating system's standard uninstaller (e.g., Add/Remove Programs on Windows).
7. **Is my data private?** Yes. Unless you enable Cloud AI or Cloud Sync, your data never leaves your hard drive.
8. **What is FSRS?** FSRS (Free Spaced Repetition Scheduler) is the mathematical algorithm that calculates when you should review cards.
9. **Can I turn off FSRS?** No, FSRS is the core engine of NeoCards, replacing older, less efficient algorithms like SM-2.
10. **How many cards can NeoCards handle?** NeoCards can easily handle over 1,000,000 flashcards without slowing down.

### Studying & Reviews
11. **What happens if I miss a day of studying?** Your cards will pile up, but the math algorithm will adjust. Just do your best to catch up!
12. **Should I study new cards or old cards first?** NeoCards defaults to showing you reviews (old cards) first, as maintaining old knowledge is more important than learning new things.
13. **Why did a card show up twice in one day?** If you press "Again", the card goes into the "Learning" queue and will be shown again in a few minutes.
14. **What does the "Bury" button do?** It hides the card until tomorrow.
15. **What does the "Suspend" button do?** It hides the card forever until you manually go into the Browser to un-suspend it.
16. **I accidentally hit "Easy" instead of "Hard"!** Press `Ctrl + Z` immediately to undo the grade.
17. **Can I review ahead of schedule?** It is not recommended, but you can create a Custom Study session.
18. **Why are my intervals growing so fast?** If you consistently hit "Easy", the algorithm assumes you know the material perfectly and pushes it months into the future.
19. **How long should I study every day?** Aim for 20-30 minutes. Consistency is better than studying for 4 hours once a week.
20. **Can I shuffle my cards?** Yes, cards due on the same day are randomized automatically.

### Creating Notes & Editor
21. **What is a Note vs. a Card?** A Note is the raw text you type. A Card is the actual flashcard generated from that Note.
22. **Can one Note make multiple Cards?** Yes! Using special templates, one Note can generate a "Forward" card and a "Reverse" card.
23. **How do I add an image?** Drag and drop the image into the editor, or copy/paste it.
24. **Where do images go?** They are copied into a special `media` folder inside your NeoCards database.
25. **Can I use audio files?** Yes, you can attach `.mp3` files to cards for language learning.
26. **How do I type math?** Wrap your equations in dollar signs (e.g., `$x^2$`).
27. **Can I change the font size?** Yes, you can highlight text and use the formatting toolbar.
28. **Does NeoCards support Markdown?** Yes, the rich text editor accepts standard Markdown shortcuts (like typing `**` for bold).
29. **What is a Cloze deletion?** A cloze is a fill-in-the-blank card. (e.g., "The capital of France is {{c1::Paris}}").
30. **How do I create a Cloze card?** Select "Cloze" from the Note Type dropdown in the editor.
31. **Can I add tables?** Yes, use the Table button in the editor toolbar.
32. **Can I change text color?** Yes, highlight the text and click the paint bucket icon.
33. **Is there a character limit?** Practically, no. You can paste entire essays, but shorter flashcards are better for learning!
34. **How do I delete a Note?** Find it in the Browser, right-click, and select Delete.
35. **If I edit a Note, do I lose my study progress?** No. Your study history is attached to the Card, not the text.

### Decks & Organization
36. **How many Decks should I have?** As few as possible. Use Tags for specific topics.
37. **How do I rename a Deck?** Right-click the deck on the Home screen and select Rename.
38. **How do I delete a Deck?** Right-click and select Delete. Warning: This deletes all cards inside it!
39. **Can I move cards between Decks?** Yes, select them in the Browser and click "Change Deck".
40. **What is a Sub-deck?** A deck inside another deck (e.g., `Science::Biology`).
41. **How do I collapse Sub-decks?** Click the small arrow next to the parent deck on the Home screen.
42. **Can I study a parent deck and get cards from all sub-decks?** Yes, clicking "Study" on a parent deck shuffles cards from all its children.
43. **What is a Tag?** A label you attach to a Note (e.g., `#midterm`).
44. **Can a Note have multiple Tags?** Yes, as many as you want.
45. **How do I rename a Tag?** Use the Tags panel in the Browser.

### Syncing & Backup
46. **How often does NeoCards sync?** Automatically in the background whenever you make a change and have internet.
47. **Can I force a sync?** Yes, click the Sync icon and press "Sync Now".
48. **What is a Sync Conflict?** When you edit the same card on two different computers while offline.
49. **How are conflicts resolved?** The most recent edit (by timestamp) is kept.
50. **Are my flashcards encrypted in the cloud?** Yes, NeoCards uses AES-256-GCM End-to-End Encryption.
51. **Can I host my own sync server?** Yes, the architecture allows for custom transport plugins.
52. **How do I export a backup?** Click File > Export.
53. **Does exporting include images?** Yes, if you choose the ".apkg" or full backup format.
54. **How do I restore a backup?** Click File > Import and select your backup file.
55. **Where is my database physically located on my PC?** In your hidden user AppData or Application Support folder.

### Importing & Anki
56. **Can I import from Anki?** Yes, export your Anki deck as `.apkg` and import it into NeoCards.
57. **Will I lose my Anki study history?** No, NeoCards imports your scheduling data and converts it to FSRS.
58. **Can I import from Quizlet?** You must export Quizlet to a CSV file first, then import the CSV.
59. **How do I import a CSV?** File > Import > select CSV. You will be asked to map the columns to Front/Back.
60. **Why are my imported images broken?** Ensure you exported the media alongside your Anki deck.

### The Browser
61. **How do I search for a specific word?** Open the Browser and type the word in the search bar.
62. **How do I find all Suspended cards?** Type `is:suspended` in the search bar.
63. **How do I find cards I learned today?** Type `rated:1` (for cards reviewed in the last 1 day).
64. **Can I sort by Due Date?** Yes, click the "Due" column header in the Browser.
65. **Can I sort by Difficulty?** Yes, click the "Difficulty" column.
66. **How do I select multiple cards?** Hold `Shift` while clicking, or press `Ctrl + A` to select all.

### Statistics & Progress
67. **What is the Heatmap?** A visual calendar showing how many cards you reviewed each day.
68. **What does Retention Rate mean?** The percentage of cards you successfully remembered.
69. **Why is my Retention Rate only 80%?** 80-90% is perfectly normal. It means the algorithm is challenging you efficiently!
70. **What is a Streak?** How many consecutive days you have completed at least one review.
71. **How is "Average Review Time" calculated?** The total time spent reviewing divided by the number of cards.
72. **What does "Mature" mean?** A card with an interval of 21 days or greater.
73. **What does "Young" mean?** A card with an interval of less than 21 days.
74. **Can I export my statistics?** Yes, the dashboard allows exporting raw data as CSV.

### AI Assistant
75. **What is the AI Copilot?** A built-in chat assistant that can help you write and understand flashcards.
76. **Does the AI read my notes?** Yes, but ONLY if you use Cloud AI, or if it runs locally on your machine.
77. **What is Local AI?** If you have Ollama installed, the AI runs entirely on your PC's graphics card. Zero data is sent to the internet.
78. **How do I generate cards with AI?** Paste text into the chat and ask it to "Generate 5 flashcards".
79. **Can the AI summarize a deck?** Yes, it uses "RAG" to read your specific deck and summarize it.
80. **Why is the AI slow?** Local AI depends on your computer's hardware. Cloud AI depends on your internet speed.
81. **Can the AI hallucinate (make mistakes)?** Yes. AI is an assistant, not a source of truth. Always double-check its flashcards!

### Plugins
82. **What is a Plugin?** An add-on created by the community to give NeoCards new features.
83. **Are plugins safe?** Yes, they run in a "Sandbox" and must ask for permission to read your database.
84. **How do I create my own plugin?** Read the Plugin SDK Developer Guide located in the NeoCards documentation.
85. **Why did a plugin crash?** Plugins are written by third parties and may contain bugs. NeoCards will disable crashing plugins automatically.

### Settings & Customization
86. **How do I turn on Dark Mode?** Gear Icon > General > Theme > Dark.
87. **Can I change the font?** Yes, in Settings > Appearance.
88. **What is "Desired Retention"?** A math setting. Higher = more reviews. Lower = fewer reviews, but you might forget more.
89. **What should my Maximum Reviews be?** Set it to 9999. You should never cap your reviews, or you will fall behind.
90. **Can I change keyboard shortcuts?** Yes, in Settings > Shortcuts.

### Troubleshooting
91. **Why is NeoCards running slow?** You may have millions of cards, or a plugin is malfunctioning. Try disabling plugins.
92. **Why didn't my sync work?** Check your internet connection, or look for a red error in the Sync Dashboard.
93. **I deleted a deck by accident!** Go to File > Restore Backup immediately. NeoCards creates automatic daily backups.
94. **Why are my cards not showing up?** Check if they are Suspended, Buried, or if you hit your Daily Limit.
95. **Images are showing up as broken links!** You may have deleted the image file, or the Sync hasn't finished downloading the media yet.

### Advanced Usage
96. **Can I use SQL to query my database?** Technically yes, using a third-party DB browser, but modifying it manually is dangerous.
97. **What is CQRS?** A software architecture term (Command Query Responsibility Segregation) used internally by NeoCards. You don't need to worry about it!
98. **Does NeoCards support RTL (Right-to-Left) languages like Arabic?** Yes, the Tiptap editor natively supports RTL.
99. **How do I run NeoCards from the command line?** You can pass arguments like `--debug` or `--portable`.
100. **Where can I get more help?** Visit the official NeoCards community forum or GitHub repository!
