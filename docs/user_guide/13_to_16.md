## Chapter 13: Import & Export

Don't let your knowledge get trapped. NeoCards makes it easy to bring flashcards in from other apps, and export them safely.

### Importing (Bringing Cards In)
1. Go to the Home Screen.
2. Click **File > Import** from the top menu (or use the Command Palette `Ctrl + K` and type "Import").
3. A File Dialog will open. Select your file.

**Supported Formats:**
- **Anki (.apkg)**: If you are moving from Anki, NeoCards will import all your decks, cards, and images perfectly.
- **CSV**: A standard spreadsheet file. Great if you make your flashcards in Microsoft Excel or Google Sheets.
- **JSON**: The native NeoCards format.

### Exporting (Backing Up)
1. Click **File > Export**.
2. Choose what you want to export (A single deck, or your entire collection).
3. Click **Export as JSON**.
- **Best Practice**: Even though NeoCards has cloud sync, you should export your entire collection once a month and save it to a USB drive or Google Drive just to be incredibly safe!

## Chapter 14: Settings

Click the Gear icon in the sidebar to open Settings.

### General Settings
- **Theme**: Choose between Dark Mode, Light Mode, or "System" (matches your computer's OS). *Recommended: Dark Mode is easier on the eyes during late-night studying.*
- **Language**: Change the language of the application interface.

### Learning Settings
- **New Cards/Day**: How many brand new concepts you want to learn each day. *Beginner Value: 20*. Do not set this to 100, or you will be overwhelmed by reviews next week!
- **Maximum Reviews/Day**: The maximum number of older cards NeoCards will force you to review. *Recommended: 9999 (No limit). You should always finish your daily reviews.*

### Advanced Settings (FSRS)
- **Desired Retention**: This tells the math algorithm how strict it should be. 
  - *Beginner Value: 90%*. This is the perfect balance between remembering well and not studying too much.
  - *Advanced Value: 95%*. Use this only if you are studying for a critical medical exam. It will force you to study twice as much.

## Chapter 15: Keyboard Shortcuts

Power users never use their mouse. Learn these shortcuts to study twice as fast!

### Global (Works everywhere)
- `Ctrl + N` (Mac: `Cmd + N`): Create a new Note.
- `Ctrl + K` (Mac: `Cmd + K`): Open the Command Palette.
- `Ctrl + ,` (Mac: `Cmd + ,`): Open Settings.

### While Studying (Review Engine)
- `Spacebar`: Show the answer.
- `1`: Grade card as **Again** (Red).
- `2`: Grade card as **Hard** (Orange).
- `3`: Grade card as **Good** (Green) (or press Spacebar again).
- `4`: Grade card as **Easy** (Blue).
- `Ctrl + Z`: Undo a grading mistake.
- `@`: Suspend the card forever.
- `-`: Bury the card until tomorrow.

### Inside the Editor
- `Ctrl + B`: **Bold** text.
- `Ctrl + I`: *Italic* text.
- `Ctrl + Enter`: Save the note.
- `Esc`: Close the editor window.

## Chapter 16: Complete Workflow Example

Let's tie it all together! Imagine you are a college student who just got home from a Biology lecture. Here is exactly what you should do:

**1. Create a Deck**
- Open NeoCards. Click **+ New Deck**. Name it "Biology 101".

**2. Create a Note**
- Press `Ctrl + N`. 
- Select "Biology 101" from the dropdown.
- In the Front box, type: *"What is the powerhouse of the cell?"*
- In the Back box, type: *"The Mitochondria."*
- Click **Save Note** (`Ctrl + Enter`). Close the editor.

**3. Study the Cards**
- Go to the Home Screen. Click "Biology 101", then click **Study Now**.
- You see *"What is the powerhouse of the cell?"*
- You think about it, then press the **Spacebar**.
- You see *"The Mitochondria."* You knew it easily, so you press **4 (Easy)**.

**4. Check Your Progress**
- Click the Graph icon in the sidebar. 
- You see your "Current Streak" just went up to 1 Day, and a green square appeared on your Heatmap!

**5. Relax!**
- The Sync icon in the sidebar spins for a second, safely backing up your progress to the cloud. You close the app and go to sleep. NeoCards will tell you exactly when you need to review that card again!
