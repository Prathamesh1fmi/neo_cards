## Chapter 18: Troubleshooting

Even the best software sometimes runs into hiccups. This chapter will teach you how to recover from common problems.

### Common Mistakes
- **Capping your Daily Reviews**: Many beginners go into Settings and limit their daily reviews to 50 because they feel overwhelmed. **Do not do this.** If you cap your reviews, the math algorithm (FSRS) breaks, and you will forget your cards. If you are overwhelmed, stop adding *new* cards, but always finish your *old* reviews!
- **Making Flashcards Too Long**: A flashcard should take 5 seconds to read. If you paste a whole paragraph on the back of a card, your brain will memorize the "shape" of the paragraph, not the actual fact. 

### Database Recovery
If your computer suddenly loses power while you are saving a note, the database is usually protected. However, if something goes completely wrong (like deleting a deck by accident):
1. Click **File > Restore Backup**.
2. NeoCards automatically creates a zip file of your database every day. Select the backup from yesterday.
3. Your database will revert to exactly how it was 24 hours ago.

### Sync Issues
- **Error: "Authentication Failed"**: You changed your password on another device, but didn't update it here. Log out and log back in.
- **Error: "Timeout"**: Your internet connection dropped. Don't panic. NeoCards will automatically try again in 5 minutes.

### Plugin Issues
If NeoCards starts freezing or acting weird, it is almost always a third-party plugin.
1. Open the Command Palette (`Ctrl + K`).
2. Type "Safe Mode" and press Enter.
3. NeoCards will restart with all plugins disabled. If the app is fast again, you know a plugin was the culprit.

## Chapter 19: Best Practices

To get the most out of NeoCards, follow these golden rules:

### 1. Study Every Single Day
The math algorithm assumes you will study exactly when it tells you to. If a card is due on Tuesday, and you don't study it until Saturday, you will likely forget it. Building a daily habit (even for 10 minutes while drinking coffee) is the secret to perfect memory.

### 2. Keep Cards Atomic
"Atomic" means one single fact per card. 
- **Bad Card**: *Front:* Tell me about George Washington. *Back:* He was the 1st President, born in 1732, crossed the Delaware, and had wooden teeth.
- **Good Card 1**: *Front:* Who was the 1st US President? *Back:* George Washington.
- **Good Card 2**: *Front:* What year was George Washington born? *Back:* 1732.
Atomic cards are easier to grade and much faster to review!

### 3. Understand Before You Memorize
Do not use the AI to generate 500 flashcards about Quantum Physics if you haven't read the textbook yet. Memorizing words you don't understand is useless. Read first, understand the concept, and *then* make flashcards to lock it into your memory forever.

## Chapter 20: Glossary

If you ever see a word in the app and don't know what it means, check here!

- **Algorithm**: A mathematical formula the computer uses to calculate your due dates.
- **Atomic**: Breaking information down into the smallest possible pieces.
- **Browser**: The giant spreadsheet screen where you can search through all your cards.
- **Bury**: To hide a card until tomorrow.
- **Card**: The actual flashcard you study (Front and Back).
- **Cloze**: A fill-in-the-blank style flashcard.
- **Deck**: A folder that holds your flashcards.
- **FSRS**: (Free Spaced Repetition Scheduler). The specific math formula NeoCards uses. It is currently the most accurate learning algorithm in the world.
- **Heatmap**: The visual grid on your Statistics page showing your daily study streaks.
- **JSON**: A computer file format. If you export your cards as JSON, you are saving the raw data.
- **Note**: The raw text you type into the editor. One Note can generate multiple Cards.
- **Plugin**: A mini-program made by the community that adds new features to NeoCards.
- **RAG**: (Retrieval-Augmented Generation). How the AI searches your database to answer questions based *only* on your notes.
- **Review**: Studying an old card you have already seen before.
- **SQLite**: The incredibly fast, offline database system running invisibly on your computer.
- **Suspend**: To hide a card forever so you never have to study it again.
- **Tag**: A label (like `#history` or `#chapter1`) you stick on a Note to make it easier to search for later.
- **UI**: (User Interface). The buttons, colors, and screens you interact with.
