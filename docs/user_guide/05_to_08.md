## Chapter 5: Creating Your First Deck

### What is a Deck?
A Deck is like a folder or a physical shoebox where you keep your flashcards. 

- **Why it exists**: To keep your subjects organized. You don't want to study Spanish vocabulary and Human Anatomy at the exact same time. 
- **When to use it**: Create a new Deck whenever you start a new subject or class.
- **When NOT to use it**: Do not create a new Deck for every single chapter of a book. Instead, create one Deck for the whole book, and use "Tags" (labels) for the chapters. This keeps your Home screen clean.

### Step-by-Step: Making a Deck
1. Go to the Home Screen (click the Book icon in the sidebar).
2. Click the big blue button that says **+ New Deck**.
3. A small dialog box will pop up asking for a Name.
4. Type "Spanish Vocab" (or whatever you are learning).
5. Click **Create**.

Your new Deck will now appear on the Home Screen!

### Sub-Decks (Nested Decks)
You can put folders inside of folders. For example, you can have a "Languages" deck, and inside it, a "Spanish" deck and a "French" deck.
- **How to do it**: When typing the name of a new deck, use two colons `::` to put it inside another deck. For example, typing `Languages::Spanish` will automatically put the Spanish deck inside the Languages deck.

## Chapter 6: Creating Notes

This is where the magic happens. A "Note" is a piece of information. NeoCards takes that Note and automatically generates flashcards from it.

### Step-by-Step: Creating a Note
1. Click the **+** icon in the sidebar (or press `Ctrl + N`).
2. At the top of the screen, click the dropdown menu to select which Deck you want this Note to go into. (Select your new "Spanish Vocab" deck).
3. You will see two large text boxes: **Front** and **Back**.
4. In the **Front** box, type: "Hello"
5. In the **Back** box, type: "Hola"
6. Click the blue **Save Note** button at the bottom (or press `Ctrl + Enter`).

Congratulations! You just created a piece of knowledge. 

### The Rich Text Editor
NeoCards does not force you to write boring, plain text. The text boxes are powerful editors, similar to Microsoft Word.
- **Formatting**: You can make text **bold**, *italic*, or underlined by highlighting it and clicking the buttons on the toolbar (or using standard shortcuts like `Ctrl + B`).
- **Colors**: Click the paint bucket icon to highlight text in yellow, red, or green. This is incredibly helpful for medical students memorizing complex pathways.
- **Images**: You can drag and drop a picture straight from your desktop into the text box. It will automatically save into your database.
- **Math**: If you are studying physics, you can type mathematical formulas. Enclose your math in dollar signs, like this: `$ E = mc^2 $`. NeoCards will render it beautifully.

### Tags
At the bottom of the editor, there is a small box called "Tags." 
- **What it does**: Tags are sticky notes you attach to your cards.
- **Why it exists**: If you want to study only the flashcards related to "Chapter 1," you can search for the `#chapter-1` tag later.
- **Example**: Type `verb`, press Enter. Type `greetings`, press Enter. Now this note has two tags!

### Autosave
- **What it does**: NeoCards automatically saves your typing every few seconds.
- **Why it exists**: So you never lose your work if your computer loses power.
- **Note**: Autosave creates a draft. You still need to click **Save Note** to officially turn it into flashcards!

## Chapter 7: Reviewing Cards

This is the most important part of NeoCards. This is where you actually study and build your memory.

### Starting a Study Session
1. Go to the Home Screen.
2. Click on the name of your Deck ("Spanish Vocab").
3. Click the big **Study Now** button.

### The Review Interface
The screen will clear away all distractions. You will only see the **Front** of your flashcard (e.g., "Hello").
- Take a moment. Try to remember the answer in your head.
- When you have an answer, press the **Spacebar** on your keyboard (or click the **Show Answer** button).

The **Back** of the card ("Hola") will appear, along with four colored buttons at the bottom of the screen. You must grade yourself on how hard it was to remember.

### The Grading Buttons
This is how NeoCards calculates the mathematical formula (FSRS) to predict when you will forget. Be honest with yourself!

1. **Again (Red)**
   - **What it means**: You completely forgot the answer, or you got it wrong.
   - **What happens**: The card will be shown to you again in a few minutes. You cannot move forward until you learn it.
   - **Shortcut**: Press `1` on your keyboard.

2. **Hard (Orange)**
   - **What it means**: You remembered the answer, but you had to think really, really hard. It took you a long time.
   - **What happens**: The card will be shown to you sooner than usual.
   - **Shortcut**: Press `2` on your keyboard.

3. **Good (Green)**
   - **What it means**: You remembered the answer with normal effort. This should be your most clicked button!
   - **What happens**: The card is pushed further into the future. If you saw it yesterday, you might not see it again for 3 days.
   - **Shortcut**: Press `3` on your keyboard, or just press the **Spacebar** again!

4. **Easy (Blue)**
   - **What it means**: The answer was instantly obvious. You barely had to think.
   - **What happens**: The card is pushed very far into the future.
   - **Shortcut**: Press `4` on your keyboard.

### Advanced Actions (Bury and Suspend)
Sometimes a card is annoying, or you don't want to study it today.
- **Bury**: Hides the card until tomorrow. Use this if you are tired of seeing the card today, but you still want to learn it.
- **Suspend**: Hides the card forever. It will never show up in your study queue again unless you manually un-suspend it. Use this if the card is a mistake or too easy to bother studying.

### Undo
- **Common Mistake**: Clicking "Again" by accident when you actually knew the answer!
- **Solution**: Press `Ctrl + Z` immediately. It will undo your grade and let you answer the card again.

## Chapter 8: Searching

As your database grows to thousands of cards, you need a way to find specific information.

### The Browser
Click the Magnifying Glass icon in the sidebar to open the Browser. 
This screen looks like a massive spreadsheet. Every row is a Note.

### Search Syntax (How to search like a pro)
There is a search bar at the top of the Browser. You can type normal words here, but you can also use special commands:
- `deck:Spanish` (Shows only cards in the Spanish deck)
- `tag:verb` (Shows only cards with the "verb" tag)
- `is:due` (Shows only cards that you need to study today)
- `front:Hello` (Searches specifically for the word "Hello" on the front of a card)

### The Inspector Panel
When you click on a row in the Browser, a panel opens on the right side of the screen.
- **What it does**: This is the Editor! You can fix typos or add images directly from the Browser without going back to the "Add Note" screen.
- **Why it exists**: For rapid editing. If you notice a spelling mistake while studying, you can find the card in the browser, fix the text in the Inspector, and keep going.

### Bulk Actions
- **What it does**: Allows you to change many cards at once.
- **How to use it**: Click the checkbox next to multiple rows. You can then click **Add Tag** to add a tag to 50 cards at the same time, or click **Change Deck** to move them all to a new folder.
